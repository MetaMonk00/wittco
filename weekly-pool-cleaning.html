Weekly Pool Cleaning in Destin, FL

Same day each week, same full checklist every visit, so the pool never gets a chance to get away from you.

What a Weekly Service Visit Actually Covers

A pool in Northwest Florida does not stay clean on its own for very long. Between the pine and oak litter, the pollen that coats everything each spring, the sand that rides in on feet and towels, and the sheer amount of sun our water takes, a week is about as long as a pool can go before the problems start compounding. Debris on the bottom stains the plaster. Leaves in the skimmer choke the flow. Chlorine burns off and algae gets its opening. Every one of those is cheap to prevent and expensive to fix after the fact.

So our weekly visit is a full checklist, not a quick skim. We net the surface and the bottom, brush the walls, steps and waterline, vacuum where vacuuming is needed, and empty the skimmer and pump baskets. We check the filter pressure and the equipment while we are there, test and adjust the chemistry, and clear anything that looks like it is heading toward a problem. You get the pool back swim-ready, on the same day each week, whether you are standing there watching or three states away.
