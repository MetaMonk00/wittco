About WittCo Pools

Over 20 years of experience keeping pools clean, clear and ready to swim in.

Two Decades of Pools, One Standard

WittCo Pools brings over 20 years of pool experience to Destin and the surrounding Emerald Coast. In that time we have seen just about everything a Gulf Coast pool can do. We have pulled a summer's worth of pine needles out of filters in Santa Rosa Beach, watched afternoon storms flip the chemistry on a beachfront pool in an hour, and been called out to plenty of rentals where nobody had touched the water in a month and a family was checking in on Friday.

What all of it taught us is that pool care is not complicated, it is just relentless. There is no product that lets you skip weeks. The pools that stay clear are the ones where somebody comes out on schedule and does the entire list every single time: skim, brush, vacuum, empty the baskets, check the equipment, test the water and correct it. That is the whole method. We are licensed and insured, we work on homes and vacation rentals throughout the area, and we would rather keep a customer for ten years than win one on a low first quote.

How We Work

The Same Full List, Every Single Visit

There is no clever shortcut in this trade. The reason a pool stays clear is that somebody works through the entire checklist on a set schedule and does not start trimming it when the season gets busy. Our visits cover the surface and the floor, the walls and the waterline, the baskets, the filter pressure, the equipment and the water chemistry. Every time.

Where judgment comes in is knowing what a specific pool needs. A screened pool in heavy shade and an open beachfront pool with twelve guests in it are two completely different problems, and treating them the same is how a service falls behind. Two decades in, we have seen enough of both to know which one we are standing in front of.

About Working With Us

WittCo Pools is a local, owner-run pool service based in Destin, Florida, built on over 20 years of hands-on pool experience. The person who quotes your pool is the same operation that services it, not a franchise or a call center.

Consistency. The most common reason people switch to us is not bad work by their old service, it is missed weeks and shrinking visits. We run the same complete checklist on the same day every week, and we tell you when something on your equipment needs attention before it becomes a bill.

Weekly service is the core of what we do because it is what actually keeps a pool right in this climate. We do take on one-time jobs like green pool recoveries and equipment repairs when the schedule allows, and plenty of those customers end up moving to weekly once they see the difference.
