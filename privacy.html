Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

WittCo Pools ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through wittcopoolpros.com and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect your name, phone number, and message details. We may also collect standard analytics data such as pages visited and device type.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

SMS / Text Messaging & Consent

If you opt in to text communications via the consent checkbox on our forms, we will only send messages related to your inquiry, quotes, scheduling, and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

We use standard web analytics tools to understand how visitors find and use this site. These tools may collect your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you view. This information is aggregated and used only to improve the website and measure the effectiveness of our advertising. We may also use conversion tracking pixels from advertising platforms to measure whether a visit resulted in a quote request. You can opt out of most tracking through your browser settings or by using an ad blocker.

Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting wittcopoolpros@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at wittcopoolpros@gmail.com or (850) 660-3394.
